# Mechanism-Level Imitation and Copyright Boundaries: The "Shell-Stripping Imitation" Protocol (Same Spirit, Not Same Form · v4.9 Upgrade)

> **Core positioning**: a production-grade path for users: "dissect the hit ➔ extract the formula ➔ reskin with zero overlap." Imitate the underlying formula of gratification (the same spirit). Never copy the original's outer shell or its wording (the same form). This eliminates at the root the sameness and infringement risk of "output that's too close to the original script."

## II. Core Formula Extraction (how to achieve "same spirit, different form")

When dissecting an original hit, never copy the original's dialogue. Use the four formula cards below to extract its core engine:

1. **Information-gap formula (who doesn't know what)**:
   - *Omniscient crushing*: the audience and protagonist know the hole card, and the villain blindly walks into the gun (the pleasure is watching a clown self-destruct);
   - *Misperception*: each side holds different information, reading the other's routine moves as a masterful plan, or taking the truth for bragging (the pleasure is the cross-wire contrast);
   - *Hidden protector*: the seemingly weak side has a dimension-crushing backer or hole card behind them. Every inch the outside villain bullies is repaid tenfold on the spot (the pleasure is instant protective payback).
2. **Chip-exchange formula (how high-level power converts into everyday gratification)**:
   - Any lofty setting (medicine, power, martial strength, prophecy, intangible-heritage craftsmanship) must, in every episode, be tied to a pressing, monetizable everyday crisis: a $7K life-saving sum, a seat in a top school district, a bankruptcy liquidation order, or a government approval memo.
   - **Formula**: `top-tier hole card / ability ➔ solves the episode's concrete everyday deadline ➔ instantly delivers visible gain + the villain pays a visible cost`.
3. **Single-episode gear-shift formula (15-second four-beat progression)**:
   - Extract the benchmark show's single-episode timing: `0-15s` set the deadline ➔ `15-45s` frontal pressure ➔ `45-85s` reveal the evidence and flip ➔ `85-110s` payoff + new crisis hook.
4. **Unit-closure formula (switch the spear every 30 episodes or fewer, without losing breath)**:
   - Extract how the benchmark show flips offense and defense around episodes 10-15 (the first-tier paywall), and how it closes the old unit around episodes 20-30 (the second-tier paywall) and detonates a new unit from the residue.

## III. Four-Step Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Imitation

1. **Step 1: Set the blacklist (shell-strip)**: list 10-20 signature shell words from the benchmark show (names, places, special tokens, original gags), and use them as this project's banned-word list.
2. **Step 2: Reskin trio (rebuild)**:
   - **Change the track / professional background** (e.g., period palace-style household intrigue ➔ a modern century-old heritage-brand inheritance fight; live-in son-in-law Dragon King ➔ retired chief chip architect);
   - **Change the core evidence carrier** (e.g., Nine-Dragon Iron Token ➔ a national top-secret patent license, or an ancestral imperial-physician gold needle set);
   - **Change the visual spectacle of the first three seconds** (never copy the original's first action in episode one; open on a high-contrast, strong image from the new profession);
3. **Step 3: Original lifeblood (prevent collisions)**: in each unit's six-tier evidence ladder, at least **two tiers** must be native plot beats derived from the new profession or background. Never map scene-by-scene onto the original's scene order;
4. **Step 4: Four-question similarity self-check (must check before submitting)**:
   - Cover the new names: can the dialogue's sentence patterns still reveal the original's shadow? (Yes ➔ rewrite with `dialogue-doctor-anti-ai.md`.)
   - Is the conflict prop in the first three seconds the same kind as the original's? (Yes ➔ change the physical action and the evidence.)
   - Do two consecutive scenes' plot events map 1:1 onto the original? (Yes ➔ shuffle and replace with native events from the new setting.)
   - Have we achieved "the same gratification tempo and the same direct dialogue, but a story that is entirely a new show"?

## IV. Copyright and Compliance Boundary Statement

This document's methodology is for lawful study of dramatic structure and for mechanism-level reference only. Ideas, genre types, and dramatic beat formulas are not copyrighted, but specific original expression is strictly protected by law. When a user pastes another person's finished-show transcript or script text and asks you to "rewrite it for me" or "just change the names and use it," the system must clearly flag the copyright risk. It must refuse sentence-by-sentence word-swap rewriting, output only the abstract breakdown tables of **Layer 2 (structural topology)** and **Layer 3 (underlying dramatic formulas)** described above, and help the user write a new, original script through the "Reskin Trio."
