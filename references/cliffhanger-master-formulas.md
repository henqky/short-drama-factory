# Hongguo Hit Cliffhangers and Paywall Stop-Point Formula Library

A short drama's soul lies in its "stop point" (cliffhanger). A top-tier stop point triggers a physical urge in the viewer to keep swiping. This module summarizes the five core cliffhanger formulas of short drama.

> **v4.0 (2026-09-28) revision**: expanded from four core formulas to **five**. Adds "Emotion Contrast Landing" (for healing dramas only). Formula 3, "Contrast Punchline Landing," gains an "everyday short punchline" subtype (in 2026-09 top punchlines got shorter and more everyday, making up 36% of cliffhanger samples). Adds a comic-drama-only "treasure claims its owner / system pop-up" cliffhanger. Source: *Lower-Tier Drama Hot-Topic Benchmark Report*, Chapter 3, §3.3.

## I. The Five Core Cliffhanger Formulas

### Formula 1: 【One Second Before the Identity Reveal】 (curiosity blowout)
* **Principle**: a core character's hidden disguise (Dragon King, tycoon, ex-wife, real heiress) is about to be seen through by the villain or the whole room. Cut at the instant of the reveal.
* **Cliffhanger action and copy**:
  * The villain snatches the contract. On the last page, the Nine-Dragon Heaven Seal stamped there makes his pupils contract. "This seal... how could it be you?!" (Black screen.)
  * The CEO impatiently tears open a DNA report. The instant he reads the words "biological daughter," he freezes in place. (Black screen.)

### Formula 2: 【Fatal Physical Danger, Frozen Mid-Air】 (survival suspense blowout)
* **Principle**: a lethal danger has already erupted. Cut hard 0.1 seconds before the harm lands.
* **Cliffhanger action and copy**:
  * A truck's headlights blaze on, bearing straight down on the heroine. The male lead dives, his fingertips just catching the hem of her coat! (Black screen.)
  * A sharp dagger pierces the protagonist's chest fabric. A single drop of blood seeps out, and the protagonist's lips suddenly curl into an eerie smirk. (Black screen.)

### Formula 3: 【Contrast Punchline Lands Hard】 (emotional peak, left hanging)
* **Principle**: when the room's mockery reaches its peak, the protagonist throws out one line that shatters everyone's worldview, and the room falls silent. 2026-09 top trend: punchlines are getting shorter and more everyday. This formula makes up 36% of cliffhanger samples and is the largest formula.
* **Subtype A: everyday short punchline (2026-09 top trend)**:
  * No more long CEO-style speeches. Instead, 3-7-word colloquial lines that cut deep through everyday realism.
  * Cliffhanger action and copy:
    * The heroine, forced out by her whole family with nothing but the clothes on her back, drags her suitcase to the door, looks back, and says quietly: "This doorway is too high for me to climb." (The room falls silent; black screen.)
    * The mother-in-law throws a tantrum demanding the daughter-in-law return the bride price. The daughter-in-law stands, looks her straight in the eye: "Then watch me die." (Four words; the mother-in-law freezes; black screen.)
    * The other woman thinks the wife won't dare make a scene. The wife lifts her teacup and smiles: "He's my husband too. And it isn't the first time." (The other woman's face goes white; black screen.)
    * A northeastern strongman is mistaken for the chamber of commerce head. The other side insists he "keep up the act," and he spreads his hands: "We agreed on one night. You want a whole month?" (Comic short line; black screen.)
* **Subtype B: long bombastic punchline (traditional, retained)**:
  * Cliffhanger action and copy:
    * The villain laughs: "Who in all of the South Province can save you?" Lin Chen slowly lifts his eyes: "The South Province? Sorry. The whole world is mine!" (The room freezes in shock; black screen.)
    * The wicked mother-in-law: "You walk out with nothing, and not one penny goes with you!" The heroine sneers and pulls out the property deed: "Old lady, look closely. This villa is in Lin Wan's name. Now get your whole family out!" (Black screen.)

### Formula 4: 【Life-or-Death / Interest Crisis: Choose One】 (extreme dilemma and moral trap)
* **Principle**: the villain forces the protagonist to choose between two equally brutal costs, with a countdown ticking.
* **Cliffhanger action and copy**:
  * "On the left, your mother's oxygen line. On the right, your sister's college acceptance letter. You have ten seconds to choose!" (A stopwatch ticks; black screen.)
  * "Sign the confession and your name is ruined. Refuse, and your daughter dies on the operating table tonight!" (Black screen.)

### Formula 5: 【Emotion Contrast Landing】 (v4.0 new · for healing and adult-romance dramas)
* **Principle**: when emotion builds to its breaking point, cut to black on a restrained gesture or line. The tension comes from feeling, not suspense, so the viewer keeps swiping. The pull comes from the emotional question "what happens next?", not from the suspense question "will he die / will the identity be exposed?". This formula fully steps outside the strong-suspense frame of the first four.
* **Applicable tracks**: adult divorce healing dramas, adult romance / healing dramas, silver-age romance.
* **Cliffhanger action and copy**:
  * The woman doesn't scream or make a scene. She arranges a tea-house meeting with her husband and the other woman. The four of them sit down. She pours herself a cup and looks up, meeting the eyes of a man at the next table who was betrayed in the same way. Their eyes lock; neither speaks. (Black screen.) *Tea-house showdown template from Timely Rain.*
  * The old woman finishes her divorce paperwork and walks out of the registry office. The man who has waited thirty years at the door asks nothing; he simply drapes his coat over her shoulders. She glances down at the coat, then looks up at him. (Black screen.)

### Appendix: comic-drama-only cliffhanger 【Treasure Claims Its Owner / System Pop-Up】 (frequent in AI comic dramas; not used in live-action)
* **Principle**: frequent in AI comic dramas, and not covered by the first four formulas. The protagonist picks up or activates a key treasure or system, and the cut lands the instant before he carries it back to his lodging or a pop-up unlocks a new feature.
* **Cliffhanger action and copy**:
  * The servant protagonist fishes a cracked basin out of the creek bed, tucks it under his arm without even rinsing it, and carries it back to the servants' quarters. The moment he pushes open the door. (Black screen.) *Treasure Basin of Immortals template.*
  * A system panel pings: "Tutorial quest complete. Unlock new feature [XX]?" The protagonist's finger hovers over the "Yes" button. (Black screen.)
* **Note**: comic-drama only; not for live-action.

## II. Cliffhanger Format Standard

At the end of every episode script, a standard 【Episode Cliffhanger】 paragraph must be listed on its own:

```text
【Episode Cliffhanger】: Chen Feng trembles as he answers his father's call. A despairing wail comes through the line: "Idiot! Who did you offend? The Chen family is finished!" Lin Chen steps forward and looks down at Chen Feng. (Black screen: swipe down to unlock Episode XX immediately.)
```
