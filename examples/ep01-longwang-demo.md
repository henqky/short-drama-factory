[Scene]: INT. Top-floor private club · night
[Cast]: Lin Chen (hidden Dragon King), Chen Feng (arrogant rich heir), Su Qingxue (fiancée)

△ Close-up: a lit cigar is ground into Lin Chen's collar, embers burning down.
△ Medium: Chen Feng slides a cup of cigar-ash water toward Lin Chen, bodyguards ringing the room.
Chen Feng (blowing smoke rings | sneering): Kneel! Drink this filthy water clean, and I'll wire you the hundred-thousand life-saving money right here.
Su Qingxue (arms folded, stepping back | cold): Lin Chen, don't be ungrateful. Know exactly how shabby your status is now.
△ Close-up: Lin Chen's lowered eyes snap up, his right fist clenching at his side, knuckles going white.
Lin Chen (very low voice): Three years ago, when your family was on its knees begging me to save them, did you wear this same face?
Chen Feng (slapping the table | sharply): You're asking to die! Cripple his hands, now!
△ Four bodyguards lunge. Lin Chen twists the leader's wrist; a bone cracks, and the man smashes through the liquor cabinet.
△ Glass shatters, liquor spills, and silence falls as Chen Feng stumbles back into a tea table.
Chen Feng (stumbling back | voice cracking): You... you dare to lay hands on me? Do you know who my father is?
△ The hotel manager rushes in, sees the broken glass, and covers his mouth.
Su Qingxue (gripping her handbag | voice tight): Lin Chen, if you walk out that door today, our engagement is over for good!
Lin Chen (brushing off his cuff | cold laugh): Engagement? When your family showed up with fake ledgers to cancel it, why didn't you bring up that word then?
△ Su Qingxue pales, grips her bag strap, and steps back behind Chen Feng.
Lin Chen (word by word): In Jiangcheng, the only people who could ever make me kneel are my dead ancestors.
△ Lin Chen slams a Nine-Dragon Iron Token on the table; the wine glasses shatter.
Lin Chen (cutting like a blade): Relay my Dragon King's order! Within ten minutes, drain every fund from the Chen Group and bankrupt them!
△ Close-up: Chen Feng stares at the nine-dragon totem, legs trembling, his phone buzzing with a call from "Father."
Lin Chen (very low voice): Three years. The thirty million your family swallowed, principal and interest, is all right here.
Chen Feng (hoarse): I... I was wrong. I'll transfer the life-saving money to you right now.
Lin Chen (coldly): Save your apologies for the bankruptcy liquidators tomorrow morning.
△ Chen Feng's knees buckle; he grips the table to keep from kneeling into the glass.
Su Qingxue (trembling): Lin Chen, we had three years together. Don't push us all the way to ruin.
Lin Chen (flatly): Affection? When that cigar was pressed down on me just now, did any of you mention affection?
△ The bodyguards exchange glances and edge toward the door.
Lin Chen (low voice): Anyone who walks out that door in ten seconds, I won't chase.
△ The bodyguards fling their batons aside and flee.
Chen Feng (roaring): Get back here! You don't even want my money anymore?
Lin Chen (looking down | cold): They just understand better than you do. From tonight, Chen family checks are scrap paper.
△ Chen Feng slumps into the spilled liquor, the "Father" call still flashing across his screen.

【Episode Cliffhanger】: Chen Feng answers, and a despairing wail comes through: "Rebel son, the bank just froze every Chen account, the Chen family is finished!" Lin Chen steps forward and closes in from above. (Black screen: swipe up to unlock Episode 2 now)
