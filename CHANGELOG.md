## v4.9 Rebuild from Real Viewer Feedback: Strong-Visual Support + 15-Second Beat Shifts + Plain-Language Payoffs + Shell-Stripping Core-Keeping Imitation (2026-10-22)

> **Background**: Across multiple viral-drama breakdowns and real viewer-feedback tests, three fatal failure modes of AI short-drama scripts were identified: ① **"the lines take forever to work out what they mean"** (the model writes subtext as literary riddles that "give physical evidence but no conclusion", or piles up untranslated industry or esoteric jargon, or drones 14-character lines at a constant pace through the whole episode); ② **"no visual support"** (one line of setting at the scene head, then a dozen-odd pure talking lines beneath, or padding with micro-actions like "eyes flicker / shifts half an inch" that cannot be shown on camera); ③ **"wrong pacing"** (the same scene spins in place within an episode, lacking a chip-shift every 15 seconds); plus "look-alike collisions" when imitating benchmark works. This release merges the **track-agnostic industrial rules** that the custom edition polished and validated into the open-source mainline, and bumps the version to **v4.9**.

## v4.3 Body-Length and Speech-Rate Recalibration (2026-09-28)

> **Background**: Live AI-generated video tests measured ≈ **15 seconds / 63 words** (4.2 words/s at the ceiling), while the old writing target of 3.3 words/s and the validator floor of 2.3 words/s were too low, so drafts came out at only 40~50% of the nominal duration (too thin).
> **Overshot once**: The targets were briefly raised based on "full-episode average = local ceiling of 3.7 words/s", but that demanded that about 90% of an episode's time be spoken, leaving no room for action, pauses, or transitions, which is exactly the "padding dialogue for density" problem.
> **Final basis (rolled back the same day)**: **63 words per 15 seconds is the local ceiling during continuous dialogue, not a whole-episode average**. Dialogue should occupy roughly 60~75% of the full episode's time, with the rest left for action, pauses, and transitions → **reference speech rate 3.0 words/s**; **E16 compares against 3.0 words/s with a tolerance widened to ±20%** (an action beat is 1.5s). Length becomes a **wide reference band** (catches only extremes): standard 320~570 / long 550~930 / comic 220~415 / long-comic 440~765 words; DLG_TARGET reference midpoints → 90s:267 / 120s:360 / 150s:447 / 180s:533 / 195s:580. **Density principle**: density is dictated by the drama (say everything in attack-defense scenes; let the visuals carry crisis and action scenes; transitions and aftershocks can be minimal). Padding is prevented by the imbalance valve E19 (continuous dialogue ≤63 words) and E21 (dialogue share ≤70%), not by raising word counts.
> **LOOP 3×5-minute stress-test patch (2026-09-29)**: Three self-run rounds of 5-minute (300-second) scripts exposed and fixed 4 rule errors: ① **E4 falsely flagged action-type episodes** (their openings are "abnormal actions" with no confrontational keywords) → added `ACTION_SIGNALS`, so action-type episodes may open with abnormal-action words; ② **action beat weight was too low** (×2.5 could not sustain 5 minutes) → **×4.0** (mixed ×2.5); ③ **E1 reference band was too high for action types** (×5.0~7.6 assumed dialogue-type) → **adjusted by mode (① 5.0~7.6 / mixed 4.2~6.5 / ② action 3.5~5.5)**; ④ **scene cap too tight for long episodes** (only 3 scenes for 300 seconds) → **scene cap now scales with episode length when duration is custom (≈1 scene per 75 seconds)**. A new T9 regression test was added (an action-type abnormal-action opening must not trigger E4).

> **Same-day addenda**: ① Length is now a **reference band derived dynamically from【Episode Length】** (duration × 3.3~5.1 words/s; a value outside the band only **warns, never blocks**. A user may say "this episode is 4 minutes" and the short-tier limit will not be applied to it; only falling below 0.5× the band, an extreme empty script, triggers FAIL); ② Added **E22 dialogue wording / typo check** (a common-misuse lookup table, now covering English misspellings and misused phrases such as "could of" → "could have"; **WARN only, never blocks**); ③ Added `references/conflict-modes.md`: **five conflict modes** (① Dialogue / ② Action-Persistence / ③ Identity-Misalignment / ④ Relationship-Emotion / ⑤ Rule-Puzzle; identifying a mode does not loosen all of them, **only ② relaxes the machine checks**). Single episodes gain a required field, `【Conflict Mode】`. Matching machine-check adaptations (per the five-mode question list): **Action mode: E9 dialogue lines halved, E21 floor 10%, E16 action-beat weight ×4.0s** (P0-1: otherwise action-mode episodes are flagged as too long for false inflation or too short for downgrade, sandwiched on both ends); **mixed-mode middle tier** (E9 8→6, E21 floor 15%, P1-1); **E23 raised to FAIL** (an action mode with no skeptic = missing opponent slot, P1-2); **N13 action-stacking exemption for action and mixed modes** (P2-1). Gap-filling: first-three-episode beat cards gain **action-mode variants**; the ledger round table gains **action-mode registration semantics** (precursor-evidence tier / preparation-and-verification tier); lazy-load matrix mode 3 mounts this file.
